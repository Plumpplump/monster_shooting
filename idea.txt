# Full Game Plan: Three-Level Shooting Challenge

## 1. Game overview

Create a mouse-controlled shooting game in **Python using Ursina**, with three increasingly difficult levels.

Players aim at enemies, shoot with unlimited ammunition, and try to survive until every enemy and boss has been defeated.

The game includes:
- A choice between **one-player and two-player modes**.
- Four enemy types.
- Three levels with increasing enemy health and score rewards.
- Player health, enemy attacks, and a scoring system.
- Start, pause, game-over, and victory screens.

**Scope for the first version:** A stationary shooting-gallery game. Players aim around the screen but do not move their characters.

> **Planning assumptions:** Hard enemies award **40 points**, since their original points value was missing. Two-player mode uses **alternating complete runs**, so both players can use the same mouse. These choices can be changed later.

---

## 2. Main game rules

1. Each player begins their run with **100 HP**.
2. Each player has a gun with **unlimited ammunition**.
3. Moving the mouse moves the aiming crosshair.
4. Pressing or holding the left mouse button fires the gun.
5. A shot damages an enemy only if the crosshair is over that enemy.
6. Each successful shot deals **one point of enemy damage**.
7. Enemies periodically attack the player.
8. Defeating enemies awards points.
9. Defeating every enemy in a level unlocks the next level.
10. Reaching zero HP ends that player’s run.
11. Clearing all three levels wins the game.

---

## 3. Player modes

### One-player mode

One player attempts all three levels.

- Starting health: **100 HP**
- Starting score: **0**
- Objective: Finish all three levels with the highest score possible.
- Losing condition: Health reaches zero.

### Two-player mode: alternating runs

Both players use the same mouse, taking turns.

1. Player 1 plays until they win or lose.
2. Their results are saved for the current match.
3. A handover screen asks Player 2 to begin.
4. Player 2 starts a fresh run with the same enemy difficulty and wave layouts.
5. A comparison screen displays both results.

Each player receives:
- Their own 100 HP.
- Their own score.
- A complete attempt at all three levels.

**Winner ranking:**
1. A player who completes the game ranks above one who does not.
2. Otherwise, the higher score wins.
3. If scores match, the higher remaining health wins.
4. If still tied, the shorter active play time wins.
5. If all these values match, declare a draw.

Pause time and handover time do not count toward active play time.

**Future expansion:** Simultaneous cooperative play with separate aiming controls. This is outside the first version because two players cannot independently aim using a single shared mouse.

---

## 4. Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Move mouse | Move the crosshair |
| Left mouse button | Fire one shot |
| Hold left mouse button | Fire repeatedly |
| Escape | Pause or resume |
| Mouse click on menu buttons | Select an option |

### Shooting behavior

- Fire immediately when the mouse button is pressed.
- While held, fire once every **0.15 seconds**.
- Do not allow extra clicks to bypass the firing cooldown.
- No ammunition counter or reload mechanic is needed.
- Each shot can hit only **one enemy**.
- If enemies overlap, hit the closest visible enemy under the crosshair.
- Shots cannot pass through enemies.

This keeps damage consistent across different clicking speeds.

## 5. Enemy statistics

### Level 1

| Enemy | Hits to defeat | Points per defeat | Damage per attack |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Easy | 1 | 10 | 10 HP |
| Medium | 3 | 20 | 13 HP |
| Hard | 5 | 40 | 17 HP |
| Boss | 20 | 100 | 30 HP |

### Level 2

Multiply Level 1 enemy health and points by three.

| Enemy | Hits to defeat | Points per defeat | Damage per attack |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Easy | 3 | 30 | 10 HP |
| Medium | 9 | 60 | 13 HP |
| Hard | 15 | 120 | 17 HP |
| Boss | 60 | 300 | 30 HP |

### Level 3

Multiply Level 2 enemy health and points by three again.

| Enemy | Hits to defeat | Points per defeat | Damage per attack |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Easy | 9 | 90 | 10 HP |
| Medium | 27 | 180 | 13 HP |
| Hard | 45 | 360 | 17 HP |
| Boss | 180 | 900 | 30 HP |

**Important:** Only enemy health and points scale by three. Attack damage stays unchanged.

The general rules are:

\[
\text{Enemy health}=\text{Base health}\times 3^{(\text{Level}-1)}
\]

\[
\text{Defeat points}=\text{Base points}\times 3^{(\text{Level}-1)}
\]

## 6. Enemy appearance and behavior

Enemies should differ by **shape, label, and color**, rather than color alone.

| Enemy | Suggested appearance | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | Small green target labeled “E” | Mostly stationary |
| Medium | Blue armored target labeled “M” | Slow horizontal movement |
| Hard | Orange angular target labeled “H” | Faster side-to-side movement |
| Boss | Large purple target labeled “BOSS” | Slow movement across a larger area |

### Shared behavior rules

- Enemies stay inside the playable area.
- They cannot spawn behind interface elements.
- They cannot attack during their entrance animation.
- Defeated enemies stop moving and attacking immediately.
- Their health bars appear above them.
- Later waves introduce tougher enemies gradually.

---

## 7. Enemy attack system

Because the player cannot move, attacks must have a mouse-based counter.

### Attack sequence

Each enemy follows this sequence:

**Enter → Wait → Warn → Attack → Recover**

During the warning stage:
- The enemy flashes.
- A visible countdown ring appears around it.
- A sound signals the incoming attack.

### Interrupting attacks

**Shoot an enemy once during its warning to cancel that attack.**

- The shot still deals normal damage.
- The enemy enters recovery.
- When recovery finishes, it can prepare another attack.
- Shots outside the warning stage do not reset its attack timer.

This gives the player two competing priorities:
- Finish damaged enemies.
- Interrupt enemies that are about to attack.

### Starting timing values

| Enemy | Wait before warning | Warning duration | Recovery after attack or interruption |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Easy | 4 seconds | 2 seconds | 3 seconds |
| Medium | 3.5 seconds | 1.7 seconds | 2.5 seconds |
| Hard | 3 seconds | 1.4 seconds | 2 seconds |
| Boss | 3 seconds | 1.8 seconds | 2.5 seconds |

Use these as initial tuning values, not final balance guarantees.

### Fairness rules

- Only one enemy may enter its warning stage at a time in the first version.
- Wait at least **0.5 seconds** between one warning ending and another beginning.
- An uninterrupted attack deals its damage once, not every frame.
- Pausing freezes all attack timers.

## 8. Level and wave structure

Each level contains four waves.

| Wave | Enemies |
|---|---|
| 1 | 4 easy |
| 2 | 3 easy and 2 medium |
| 3 | 2 medium and 2 hard |
| 4 | 1 boss |

Each level therefore contains:
- **7 easy enemies**
- **4 medium enemies**
- **2 hard enemies**
- **1 boss**
- **14 enemies total**

The complete game contains **42 enemies**, including three bosses.

### Spawning rules

- Maximum of **three regular enemies visible at once**.
- Spawn remaining enemies when a visible enemy is defeated.
- Give newly spawned enemies a **one-second entrance period** before they can attack.
- Run the boss wave alone.
- Begin the next wave after all enemies from the current wave are defeated.
- Display a two-second wave announcement between waves.

### Difficulty progression

- **Level 1:** Introduces aiming, shooting, and attack interruption.
- **Level 2:** Uses the same structure with tougher enemies and moderately faster movement.
- **Level 3:** Uses maximum enemy health and faster movement, while keeping attacks readable and interruptible.

Do not add extra enemies or increase attack damage until the original version has been playtested.

---

## 9. Health and recovery

- Start each run with **100 HP**.
- Maximum health is **100 HP**.
- Health persists between waves.
- After completing Levels 1 and 2, restore health to **100 HP**.
- There are no health pickups in the first version.
- At zero HP, immediately stop gameplay and show the game-over screen.

Restoring health between levels makes each level a separate survival challenge without requiring a flawless full-game run.

---

## 10. Boss encounters

Every level ends with one boss.

### Boss rules

- Display a large boss health bar at the top of the screen.
- Show both remaining and maximum health.
- Use a larger model than regular enemies.
- Give attacks distinctive warning effects.
- Keep the boss vulnerable throughout the encounter.
- Allow attack interruption using the same rule as regular enemies.

### Boss progression

- **Level 1 boss:** Mostly stationary.
- **Level 2 boss:** Moves slowly from side to side.
- **Level 3 boss:** Moves faster and changes direction more often.

The final boss requires **180 hits**. At one shot every 0.15 seconds, that is approximately **27 seconds of continuous accurate shooting**, so playtesting should check whether the encounter remains engaging.

---

## 11. Scoring and statistics

### Score rules

- Award points only when an enemy is defeated.
- Do not award points for individual hits.
- Misses do not subtract points.
- Do not add survival, speed, or accuracy bonuses in the first version.

### Maximum scores

Using the planned enemy counts:

| Level | Available points |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 330 |
| 2 | 990 |
| 3 | 2,970 |
| **Full game** | **4,290** |

### Track these statistics

- Total score
- Current level and wave
- Enemies defeated
- Shots fired
- Successful hits
- Accuracy
- Active play time
- Remaining health
- Whether the player completed the game

\[
\text{Accuracy}=\frac{\text{Successful hits}}{\text{Shots fired}}\times 100\%
\]

If no shots have been fired, display accuracy as **0%**.

## 13. Visual and audio style

Use a simple, non-graphic arcade style.

### Visual feedback
- Brief muzzle flash when firing.
- Enemy flash when hit.
- Small hit marker at the crosshair.
- Floating points when an enemy is defeated.
- Brief screen-edge flash when the player takes damage.
- Simple particle effect when an enemy disappears.

### Audio
- Gunshot
- Successful hit
- Enemy defeat
- Attack warning
- Player damage
- Boss entrance
- Level completion
- Victory and defeat

Sound must be optional. Warnings must remain understandable without audio.

Avoid strong screen shake or rapid flashing by default.

## 14. Technical organization

Use Ursina for the game, with the logic separated into small systems.

| Component | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Game controller | Game state, mode selection, run progression |
| Player state | Health, score, statistics |
| Weapon | Firing input, cooldown, hit detection |
| Enemy | Health, movement, attack states, defeat |
| Wave manager | Enemy spawning and wave completion |
| Attack coordinator | Prevents overlapping attack warnings |
| Interface manager | Menus, HUD, results |
| Audio manager | Sound playback and mute setting |
| Configuration | Enemy statistics, timing, wave definitions |

### Main game states

```text
MAIN MENU
    ↓
MODE SELECTION
    ↓
PLAYER READY
    ↓
PLAYING ↔ PAUSED
    ↓
LEVEL COMPLETE → NEXT LEVEL
    ↓
GAME OVER or VICTORY
    ↓
PLAYER 2 READY, if required
    ↓
FINAL RESULTS
```

Store enemy values and wave definitions in configuration data rather than scattering them throughout the code.

When restarting a run, clear all enemies, timers, effects, and previous statistics before creating the new run.

## 16. Testing checklist

### Shooting
- A miss causes no damage.
- A valid hit removes exactly one enemy health point.
- Holding fire respects the cooldown.
- One shot cannot damage multiple enemies.
- Defeated enemies cannot receive additional hits or award duplicate points.

### Enemy attacks
- Damage matches the enemy’s type.
- Warning shots interrupt attacks.
- Attacks cause damage only once.
- Only one warning occurs at a time.
- Enemies cannot attack after defeat.

### Progression
- Every wave spawns the correct enemies.
- Levels advance only after all enemies are defeated.
- Level 2 values are three times Level 1 values.
- Level 3 values are nine times Level 1 values.
- Health restores between levels.
- Clearing Level 3 triggers victory.

### Player modes and menus
- Player 2 starts with fresh health and statistics.
- Both players receive equivalent enemy layouts.
- Pausing freezes gameplay and active play time.
- Restarting leaves no enemies or timers from the previous run.
- Returning to the menu stops the current game.

## 17. First-version boundaries

**Include:** Three levels, four enemy types, unlimited shooting, interruptible attacks, health, scoring, menus, and alternating two-player mode.

**Save for later:** Simultaneous co-op, online multiplayer, player movement, weapon upgrades, pickups, extra enemy types, and saved leaderboards.

**Definition of done:** A player can launch the game, select a mode, complete or lose a full run, view correct results, and restart without errors.

For two person playing, you can take a look at the existing networking code at p2p/ You could reuse this module or create your own.

Use modern software architecture.

Latest ursina API reference is at https://www.ursinaengine.org/api_reference_v8_2_0/index.html.

Also create a README.md for the purpose of the game, the requirements of installation, and how to play in the end.
